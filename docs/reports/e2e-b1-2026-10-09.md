# E2E case B1 — sale to cash — 2026-10-09

Plan: [../specs/t1-playwright-test-plan.md](../specs/t1-playwright-test-plan.md), part B1. Test: `frontend/e2e/b1-sale-to-cash.spec.js` (helpers: `frontend/e2e/flow.js`). Run: `pnpm run e2e -- e2e/b1-sale-to-cash.spec.js`.

## Result

**The whole chain works**, at the three screen sizes (desktop 1280, tablet 820, phone 375): 3 / 3 pass. One tab per user, every step done through the screens:

1. Sale Worker: quotation (customer C001, RF-2D x 2, price 64,000 from the price list) → send → accepted → sale order → request confirmation
2. Sale Manager: approves; Sale Worker: send to factory
3. Factory Worker: work order for what waits (plan dates) → Factory Manager: schedule
4. Factory Worker: the four production steps on the tracking board → Factory Manager: finish
5. Factory Worker: two serial numbers (one press each), transfer to the warehouse → Factory Manager: transfer
6. Sale Worker: mark ready (accepted only because the goods are in the warehouse) → delivery note → names the two units → confirm delivery
7. Accounting Worker: invoice from the delivery note → Accounting Manager: issue → Accounting Worker: receive payment (total ¥140,800 = 2 x 64,000 + 10 % tax) → confirm → the invoice is **Paid**
8. Sale Manager: close the order
9. Traceability: the serial number shows the work order, the sale order, the delivery note and the invoice
10. The books agree: no negative quantity in the stock, no finished refrigerator left on hand

The opening stock of the materials is put in by a helper (a Worker makes the adjustments, a Manager approves them), not by clicking six times.
